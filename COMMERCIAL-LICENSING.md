# WORTH Commercial Licensing

WORTH is publicly available under the Business Source License 1.1. The public
license permits production use when the combined annual revenue of the user and
its affiliates is below US $10 million. Organizations at or above that threshold
need a commercial license for production use.

Evaluation, development, testing, modification, forking, and redistribution
remain available under the public license. The commercial license supplies the
additional production-use grant; it does not require a separate codebase.

## Expected enterprise grant

The intended commercial agreement is simple and organization-wide. Subject to
the signed agreement and payment, it can cover:

- unlimited employees, applications, users, and production deployments;
- internal modifications and private forks;
- controlled affiliates and later-acquired entities;
- development, testing, disaster recovery, and production use; and
- optional support or engineering services under a separate order form.

The commercial agreement does not ordinarily meter cores, requests, seats, or
deployments. Reselling WORTH itself as a standalone product or service requires
an express grant.

## Revenue calculation

The controlling definition is in [LICENSE](./LICENSE). In summary, the threshold
uses consolidated worldwide gross revenue across entities under common control.
It cannot be evaluated separately by division, subsidiary, shell company, or a
contractor operating on behalf of the production user.

An organization that crosses the threshold after qualifying for free production
use receives the 90-day transition described in the license.
