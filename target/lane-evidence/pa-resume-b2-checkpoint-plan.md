# B2 checkpoint and recovered-transition consumer slice

Base: `e4d3b01c8d`. This is a coherent subfamily of B2, not a claim that
the remaining durability or other adapter consumers have been closed.

The governing boundary is the existing per-claim map at
`landing2a-durability-contract-claim-disposition.md` and the coordinator's
Landing 2a disposition: public graceful semantics stay on actual public owners;
private resident, allocation and pin observations move to C2; injected fates
move to C4. No new provider, discovery helper or authority door is introduced.

Read-only independent boundary audits were requested for the three B2 families.
They confirmed that the selected semantics have existing public surfaces:
branch-reference targets and lifecycle postures, admitted snapshots, observed
index generations, captured Native restoration, and recovered checkpoint
transition ownership. Existing nearby courts omit distinct assertions, so the
original assertions are ported rather than treating those courts as substitutes.

  basis versus moved basis; self-loop adjacency and projected adjacency; foreign
  runtime-name refusal with no installed commits.
- Two exact branch-target recovery courts: equal selected sibling targets and
  readable contents; divergent checkpoint-plus-tail branch contents.
- Five lifecycle/transition journeys: fork before first target commit, covered
  fork after source movement, transition-tail ordering/artifact equality,
  lifecycle/retired-name recovery, independent byte-identical histories.
- Two logical/native checkpoint courts: exact one-checkpoint/one-tail coverage,
  original receipt and earlier envelope; original observed index generation.
- Three recovered-transition courts: exact successor admission; foreign
  recovery/candidate denial and returned-authority retry; moved source/stale
  candidate denial with no extra commit.
- The Native builder-invariant configuration unit remains inside its owner on
  genuine Memory, preserving the exact rule ID and unsealed configuration.

`Arc<TestDurableMedium>` before recovery. Public assertions use actual admitted
