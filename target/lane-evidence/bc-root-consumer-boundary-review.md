Read-only independent review; no source edits, builds or tests. The three granted consumer paths passed the coordinator's committed and dirty Lifecycle preflight, as observed by the implementer. This review does not certify execution or full adapter removal.

## Findings

1. **P1 — granting the consumer files removes their ownership obstacle, but does not make their retained-root claims publicly observable.** `branch/root_tests.rs::publication_cost_distinguishes_new_and_reused_schema_authority` asserts exact new/reused schema-authority counts and identical allocation IDs across two genuine published roots. The actual methods and root are private in `branch/root.rs` (`publication_cost`, `schema_authority`) and the allocation/reuse decision is in `branch/root_capture_preparation.rs::prepare_schema` / `PreparedRootSchema::apply_cost`. The public sharing observation reports aggregate authoritative bytes and touched/reused regions, not these counts or the schema allocation ID. Equal schema commitments do not establish shared allocation. Creating an issuer and a synthetic previous root is not an equivalent pure-value test: the issuer has live counters and the original claim is about real publication. A direct unit for `apply_cost` could retain its pure arithmetic only after an owner-level production split; it would not by itself replace the publication assertion.

2. **P1 — mutation readmission and completeness assert a private owner boundary, not just the bytes' integrity.** `relink_rejects_schema_authority_mutation`, `relink_rejects_visibility_tuple_mutation`, the relink half of `visibility_commitment_binds_every_visible_tuple_axis`, and the strategy/schema tests in `root_authoritative_payload_tests.rs` retain an authentic root and submit a different envelope to `branch/root_readmission.rs::relink_canonical_envelope`. The exact schema/visibility denial is after receipt, schema, current-content and bound-visibility checks. Public artifact `readmit` verifies frame, canonical form and original-input consistency before public recovery reaches roots. Tampering/resealing provider bytes is useful public evidence, but an earlier artifact/root-linkage denial cannot prove these later checks or their precise refusal. `completeness_recomputes_visibility_commitment` also mutates a clone's private committed visibility while preserving its real original evidence, then invokes `branch/root.rs::is_complete`; public admitted-basis descriptors cannot install that altered owner state. None of these are among grant 12's five deliberately retired private claims.

3. **P1 — the funded root DATA assertions remain owner-private after provider migration.** `root_tests/request.rs::request_readmission_matches_real_root_and_keeps_only_actual_retained_owners` calls `branch/root_readmission/request.rs::{readmit_for_request,readmit_unchanged_for_request}` over the genuine published root, compares exact axes/descriptor/count, asserts `Arc::ptr_eq` of the region collection, and measures release after dropping the exact reconstructed aliases. `request_root_refusal_preserves_original_and_releases_partial_backing` uses a root-size-derived pressure boundary to demand the exact DATA constructor stop, then checks zero partial charge and unchanged original axes. Whole public recovery funding has additional owners/lifetimes and cannot establish these exact DATA allocations or the same-cell pointer equality. A separate public recovery ledger court remains useful, but does not alone preserve these assertions.

4. **Lawful partial splits exist and should not be treated as blanket blockers.** The hash-sensitivity half of `visibility_commitment_binds_every_visible_tuple_axis` is pure descriptive meaning: a root-free value envelope/body fixture can supply each axis to the existing `RelationalBranchVisibilityCommitment::for_root` without a request, runtime or issuer. Its root-relink half still falls under finding 2. The request-free buffered-preimage arithmetic in `streamed_visibility_preserves_original_buffered_preimage_and_request_stop` can likewise remain value-only. The real request-funded streaming/cancellation comparison requires `ExecutionRequest` and currently calls the production-private `root_visibility.rs::for_root_for_request`; it belongs in a court only through an honest production DATA owner split or existing public flow whose exact streaming subject can be established. The memory-only merge test has no local-provider dependency and should not be moved merely because it shares a helper file. The provider-dependent strategy/schema publication setup can use public external-provider transactions and public schema transitions, but their retained private-root assertions still need findings 1–3 resolved.

5. **The external provider cannot repair a Native-private owner interface.** `worth-testkit-relational` implements the normal dependency's Native trait. Native's unit-test compilation has a distinct trait/type identity. A local bridge, generic conversion, renamed attachment or macro does not cross that boundary honestly. A new root getter, mutable root hook, private DATA-constructor port or a receiver-owned test door would be a new observer surface expressly rejected by grant 12. No such change is proposed here.
