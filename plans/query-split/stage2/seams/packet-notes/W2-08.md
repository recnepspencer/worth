# W2-08 — Attempt fact and decision-fact store vocabulary below the provider

Base: `e200ba0e00`. Edited without compiling (no cargo); the checks are the Python
simulations and the scripts' dry runs. Design first, then what was done.

## Design

### The direction and the destination

Under the host order the seams run provider (47) / application_checkpoint (49) /
output_lineage (55) / invariant_projection (56) / application_output_demand (57) ->
application_attempt (60). The items go below all five. The only place the attempt owns below
the provider is its vocabulary, `primary_graph::vocabulary::application_attempt`: no attempt-owned
child of `primary_graph` sits between the observation families (25-35) and the provider (47),
and a new child would be unranked by the host order and land at the top. So the rule for every
moved item is the vocabulary rule: what lands in `vocabulary` names nothing in a
non-vocabulary family of the primary graph (otherwise `vocabulary` joins the strongly connected
component and the seam reappears as `X -> vocabulary`).

### Traits that stay, and dispatch

- `CompletedFactProjectionRouting` (attempt, `read_set::application_projection`): `build` and
  `refresh` drive completion over the attempt's observed facts. Implemented for the one concrete
  type and called statically: neither `dyn` (no caller needs erasure, and `build` has no
  receiver) nor a generic bound (no caller is generic). Its bodies are concrete code compiled
  once, so iteration law 7 holds without a shell.
- No trait is needed for the denial cluster: every impl there is pure refusal construction and
  classification, so the units move whole.
- `SourcePublicationRead` needs no trait: its two observation-taking functions only read the
  observation's address, which output_lineage already exposes; the callers (one in
  invariant_projection, one in the attempt's read set) pass the address. Two call-site lines,
  the comparison unchanged (`Arc::ptr_eq` on the same address).

### Ownership notes

- `relational_execution_denial` goes to the provider's vocabulary, not the attempt's denial
  (contracts.md Family B step 4 said the attempt's denial "where 4 of 5 callers are"). Today it
  has 15 callers across provider, contribution, checkpoint and resolution, and it is "the only
  conversion from Relational execution causes to Query denial kinds"; its owner stays the
  provider and the module moves whole with its unit tests. The facade keeps
  `relational_execution_kind`.
- The facade keeps `WorthQueryApplicationAttemptDenial`, `WorthQueryApplicationSourceAdjacencyEndpoints`
  and `WorthQueryApplicationFactKey`; they re-export from the new paths.
- W2-09's items (commit and registration vocabulary) are not touched.

## What was done

Script output (in order), then hand fixes, all without cargo:

1. `module_move.py` `provider::relational_execution_denial` -> `vocabulary::provider::relational_execution_denial`
   (with its `preflight` test module and `tests.rs`).
2. `module_move.py` `application_attempt::denial` -> `vocabulary::application_attempt::attempt_denial`
   (13 files; `unobserved_field_write` widened from `pub(in ..::application_attempt)` to
   `pub(in ..::primary_graph)`). The empty `denial/kind.rs` shell (its kind already lives in
   `domain_computation::vocabulary::primary_graph::kind`) was deleted with its `mod` line.
3. `unit_move.py` `retained_decision_facts::StoreDenial` -> new `vocabulary::application_attempt::retained_decision_facts`
   (type, `From`, `Display`, `Error`, `into_attempt_denial`, `ChargedBytes`, and its door).
4. `module_move.py` `retained_decision_facts::control` and `::endpoints` under that module.
5. `unit_move.py` `CompletedFactProjection` -> `vocabulary::application_attempt::application_projection`,
   then by hand: `build`/`refresh` back into `read_set/application_projection.rs` as
   `CompletedFactProjectionRouting`; the vocabulary type gained `from_routing` (the one
   constructor, `count` derived from the scopes) and a `scopes()` reader. The two call-site
   files import the trait.
6. By hand: `SourcePublicationRead::new` takes the publication address and `admits_observation`
   became `admits_address` (`Arc::ptr_eq` unchanged); call sites in
   `invariant_projection/operation_reader/output_availability.rs` and `read_set/completion.rs`
   pass `observation.address()`.
7. New vocabulary families `vocabulary::{structural_walk, aggregate_observation, component_selection}`;
   `module_move.py` of `structural_walk::key` (its `key_ordering_tests.rs` moved under `key/`
   first, `#[path]` adjusted), `aggregate_observation::{selection, member_work, key}`,
   `component_selection::key` (its `use super::*` replaced by the three external imports it used).
8. `unit_move.py` `WorthQueryApplicationFactKey` (with its `ChargedBytes` impl and the `bytes`
   helper, now `pub(in ..::primary_graph)`) into `vocabulary::application_attempt::fact`, and
   `SourcePublicationRead` into `vocabulary::application_attempt::source_publication_read`. One
   spurious import the script added (`SourceReadAuthorization`, matched from a variant name) was
   removed; the emptied `fact/source_publication_read.rs` was deleted.
9. Hand tidying: `mod` lines the scripts placed above inner docs moved below them; the per-item
   rewrite of the facade's grouped `application_attempt` re-export restored to the grouped line
   with two explicit lines for the moved names; unused imports left by the moves removed.
10. `src_court_doors.py items`; three door files no `mod` reaches any more deleted
    (`aggregate_observation/selection`, `application_attempt/retained_decision_facts/control`,
    `provider/relational_execution_denial`).

Checks: line caps (`diff e200ba0e00`) pass, 537 files; facade export names identical to the base
(15 facade files); no moved vocabulary file names a non-vocabulary primary-graph module.

## Not done, and why

- `WorthQueryApplicationObservedFact` (58 references over the five seams). Splitting the impls
  off is not enough: the enum's own data names `ObservedSourcePublication` (output_lineage, above
  the provider; its data names application_query's `SourceQueryReadClosure` and
  invariant_projection's `ObservedOutputCandidate`), `ManagedSourceRead` -> access_strategy's
  `CapturedManagedInfluence` (holding the `RetainedManagedInfluence` trait), `ComponentSelection`
  (held back) and `AggregateObservation` -> `SourceFactsAllocation` (held back, merges.md section
  2 steps 4-5). Lowering it now would make `vocabulary` name output_lineage, access_strategy,
  component_selection and aggregate_observation and pull it into the component. Prerequisites:
  Family C's `ObservedSourcePublication` move, the source-facts allocation step, and a ruling for
  access_strategy's managed-influence types.
- `CompletedOriginalRead` (4). Its data holds `FactPayload` (application_checkpoint, whose own
  impls store observed facts) and `WorthQueryApplicationSnapshotLease` (merges.md section 2 steps
  4-5). It follows the observed fact and the snapshot lease.
