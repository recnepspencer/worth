## Owner decisions that differ from the brief's wording

1. **`SharedSelectedProductOperation` stays in product_operation.** It wraps
   `WorthQuerySelectedProductOperation`, which the ruling keeps in product_operation;
   moving the wrapper down would put the selection's type in an attempt field (a new
   upward reference). The draft-stack run moved both together, which the ruling
   excludes. Instead the snapshot lease takes parts:
   `WorthQueryApplicationSnapshotLease::from_shared_selected(application, product,
   application_basis, selection_origin)` and `retain_selection_origin_from(product,
   origin)`; product_operation calls them (`SharedSelectedProductOperation::share_snapshot_lease`,
   `operation.rs`). The one authorization caller now calls `shared.share_snapshot_lease()`.
2. **`HistoryProducerInvocation` stays in product_operation; the attempt takes a port.**
   Its impls name `WorthQueryHistoryNavigationDenial`, `current_head`,
   `ProductOperationHost` and `HistoryProducerCutover`, so it cannot move whole (lowering
   `HistoryAuthorityLifetime` alone does not help). The attempt's only use of it was
   binding it to its own admitted request and handing the cutover to the provider. That is
   now the attempt-owned `dyn` port
   `application_attempt::provider_execution::entry::HistoryProducerCommit`
   (`commit_prepared_application(provider, session, producer_request, policy)`),
   implemented for `HistoryProducerInvocation` in `producer_invocation.rs`. The attempt
   carries `Option<&dyn HistoryProducerCommit>`; the two callers above it (the
   contribution producer commit and the runtime facade forwarder, whose public
   signature is unchanged) coerce once.
3. **The workflow preparation blocks: logic stays under the attempt's seal, entries move
   up.** The eight blocks' bodies open the attempt's sealed read set
   (`read_set.lease`, `read.facts`, sealed authority structs built with private fields).
   Hosting them in product_operation would have widened those fields. Their only callers
   were the workflow publication adapters, whose public `&WorthQuerySelectedProductOperation`
   parameter is what really pins them above the attempt. So:
   - The attempt owns `application_attempt::workflow::selection_basis::WorkflowSelectionBasis`,
     the parts one preparation reads: the runtime as `&dyn ApplicationAttemptHost`, the
     product lease and the selected-program inspection. The seven attempt blocks became
     `impl WorkflowSelectionBasis` blocks with unchanged bodies (transition preparation and
     observation, instance preparation, proposal preparation, definition preparation and
     retirement). Transition approval now takes `installed` from its caller, since the
     vocabulary's `workflow_spec_for` needs the selection.
   - product_operation owns `product_operation::workflow_preparation` (one child per
     program: `transition`, `instance`, `proposal`, `captured_input`, `definition`) and
     `WorthQuerySelectedProductOperation::workflow_selection_basis()`.
   - All seven adapters moved up whole (each unit with every impl): advance, instance,
     birth, succession, proposal, definition publication and definition retirement. Thin
     method bodies moved verbatim. Bodies that build sealed attempt authority stay in the
     attempt as functions over the basis and the adapter method hands them the basis:
     `prepare_local_action`, `complete_local_operation`, `prepare_birth`,
     `prepare_succession`, and the actor-context binders (now associated functions of
     `WorthQueryWorkflowActorContext`, taking `runtime: u64` and `installed`).
   - The one public block, `prepare_workflow_captured_input`, moved up verbatim as an
     inherent method in the type's own module. The four `PreparedWorkflowCapturedInput`
     fields it writes went from `pub(super)` to `pub(in primary_graph)`, matching their two
     sibling fields; the `Scope`/`Query` aliases are `pub(in primary_graph)`.
   - `WorkflowCapturedInputs::prepare` took the selection publicly. It is now
     `pub(in primary_graph)` over the basis, and the public entry is the selection's
     `prepare_workflow_captured_inputs` (beside `prepare_workflow_captured_input`). The
     one caller in `worth-query-publication` was updated.
   - The definition publication door (`prepare_workflow_publication_for_test`) moved with
     its entry to `product_operation/workflow_preparation/definition/court_doors_for_test.rs`;
     the court that calls it is unchanged.
