## R1 boundary review

RelationalCommitEnvelopeResidency owns cold journal demand and physical damage
caching. Its readmission already returns RecoveryStop::Content with the exact
Native denial, but demand_read discards that payload as physical Damaged. The
private residency failure then feeds public history doors through the Copy,
serialized RelationalExecutionDenialCause. A direct Content(Denial) cause would
be recursively sized because Denial already contains Execution(Cause).

Native content owns a non-execution semantic rejection payload. One closed
variant declaration generates the existing flat Denial spelling and its Copy,
serialized Rejection payload, keeping semantic cases and fields synchronized.
Residency carries Content(Denial); execution carries Content(Rejection), appended
after existing variants; recovery reconstructs the original semantic denial.
Execution(cause) passes through unchanged. No content rejection is promoted to
physical proven damage or a synthetic kernel/resource stop. This adds no
authority, byte store, generic engine body or AspectShape form.

The same reviewer independently approved this carrier boundary before code.
