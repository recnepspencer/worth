names are admitted once; immutable proposal readmission repeats registry/topology
checks without rebuilding a name set on the warm path.

prepare_nested_projection consumes a genuine current parent read and occurrence,
then prepares the direct child at its actual endpoint. Actual ancestor relation
records and sealed issuance origin distinguish convergent paths even when root,
scope, route, anchor and physical leaf record coincide. The existing Native
prepared backing additionally checks its real invariant view or selected image.
No coordinate/digest/Arc by itself grants a Source read or principal READ.

The flat lineage Vec and Arc metadata are original-funded before allocation.
The lineage retains the existing invariant meter or an ExecutionMemoryReservation
from the same actual selected request; its records drop before the original payer.
Root routes retain None ancestry and allocate no lineage. No ExecutionRequest,
second allowance, graph mirror or recursive lineage-drop chain is retained.

Native scheduling collects affected immediate parents and iteratively pulls them
through real ancestor routes on BOTH images, keeping empty adjacency support and
original typed stops. Scheduling is conservative; no exact-relevance claim follows.
Physical direct occurrences, rather than deduplicated composed endpoints, enumerate
the tree. Existing authored-field/lifecycle inventory already visits every route.
Query's compiler/content/equality/footprint receivers continue their explicit nested
refusal until the next complete receiving slice. No competing ordinary Query path
or new public CAD application is added.
