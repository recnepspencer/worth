## Result and eligibility

An ordinary complete maintained Parent with the installed tracked full-Source
projection profile may retain qualified candidate influence. It retains the
original complete cut, every genuine candidate Entry (including exact rejects),
full admitted parameters, mode, installed Parent/Source/upper bound and actual
Native Source observation. Existing public admission/disclosure still precedes
capture; the operation's fact/READ/effect admission still governs consumption.

The private consistency verifier traverses only the exact target Source using
the same installed candidate/exact evaluator under a real retained-verification
audience. It verifies a fresh complete cut and compares full-generation candidate
membership and all genuine original Entry bodies. Unchanged immutable bodies
prove the complete projected inputs unchanged, including tracked root predicates,
nested endpoints and ordering inputs. Empty results still retain predicate/absence
coverage. Returned rows, a digest, physical Node IDs and candidate assertions are
insufficient.

Irrelevant edits to noncandidate Source rows can then preserve a genuine old
expectation through a real mutation. Matching entrants, a candidate body change,
foreign installation/Native cell, missing coverage or interrupted comparison
cannot establish equality. Non-shared Entry allocations mean equality was not
proved; they do not assert unequal values. Unsupported shapes retain the existing
whole-Source posture. No public rows/availability are exposed by consistency
verification, and no old grant becomes reusable READ.

The observed Source exposes descriptive `has_verified_realized_footprint()` so
consumer evidence can distinguish this supported posture from conservative Source
execution. That boolean cannot construct or transfer the legality witness.
Existing `.expect_source` / `.expect_result_set` remain the mutation entrances:

```rust,ignore
let old = request.query(find("A", "sh")).execute()?;
assert!(old.result_set_observation()?.has_verified_realized_footprint());
// A separately committed unrelated Source row must not poison this influence.

    .expect_result_set(old.into_result_set_observation()?)
    .idempotency(&key).execute_in_program(&world, allocation)?;
```

Full Source revision remains part of the current observed-Source semantic identity
until the next reuse slice. Do not claim new output-demand identity stability from
this expectation/currentness result alone.
