## Scope

owns facade contraction, developer guidance, consumer cutover, workaround
deletion, and permanent residue enforcement. It does not create another Query
runtime, authorization lane, scheduler, provider path, or replay surface.

The Phase 9 production candidate and its
[closure ledger](./milestone-9.16-runtime-phase-9-closure-ledger.md) are the
entry proof. Bank Phase 6 remains downstream until every row below is proved.

## Closure Rule

`PROVED` requires the production owner, a public positive witness, an
independent enforcement boundary, and the exact verification command. A green
suite cannot close a row whose documentation, deletion, or consumer evidence
is absent. `OPEN` and `DEFECT` block Bank Phase 6.
