  initial_adjustment/connection.rs turn them into output demands. Preserve this

## Required result and boundary constraints

Actual spatial discovery must operate on a real maintained face universe, include
indirect vertex motion, use genuine full-generation Source occurrences and the
same ordinary managed provider/invariant extension. Numerical boxes are candidate
bounds; Native contour eligibility and exact contact remain independent owners.
No covered per-result graph lookups, all-component preparation before every box
query, manually preselected collision links, private cache or freshness registry
can establish the intended locality claim.

The1000/2000 chain and discovery beside50000 unrelated faces must include actual
setup/scope work and distinguish discovery, candidate/refinement/delivery counts,
maintenance and cleanup. The existing paired Catalog edge-maintenance court does
not discharge this face-discovery requirement.

## Design decisions under review

1. Established by the Scene/Face slice: optional SceneFaceContribution in
   topology_entry supplies real Scene and Face identities. A Face owns an anchor into the existing Native
   PlanarSuccessor contour; a supported triangular-face contract must be checked
   against the actual closed contour, not inferred from three coordinates or a
   BodyKey proxy. A Scene-to-Face relation supplies the actual universe. Vertex
   coordinates remain on Body; boxes are derived and never become geometry truth.
2. Established by the typed-box slice: one IntegerBox2<Space> owner retains Box2
   as its concrete signed specialization and adds the full-u64 ModelLocalMetre
   class. The same box/tree/contact algorithms serve both without conversion,
   epsilon, clamp or duplicated geometry. SceneFaceRow::bounds() is the actual
   unsigned consumer. Model-instance affinity remains with Scene/context custody.
3. Established by the Scene/Face slice: the existing ConsumerSchema owner adopts
   a fourth contribution, now at minor1.3 with its actual provider, preserving v1 query meanings and using the
   matching host tuple and actual program roster. No new schema is created solely
   for the size court, and no competing declaration authority is introduced.
