# Milestone 9.22: Occurrence-Safe Stage And Subartifact Reuse

## Goal

Reuse eligible stages and subartifacts across attempts through framework-owned
sharing and lifecycle authority while preserving semantic equivalence,
production/acquisition occurrence, independent-certification purpose,
dependencies, provider posture, governance, and resource ownership.

## Roadmap Placement

Milestone 9.15 established artifact and occurrence identity and declared
reproducibility. Milestones 9.19 through 9.21 add advanced access, execution,
footprint, and decision evidence. This milestone independently owns their reuse
lifecycle. Reuse closes last because it may substitute only artifacts whose
complete meaning is already settled; it is not a Milestone 9.17 branch phase.

## Adversarial Constraint

Equal-content artifacts arise from distinct required observations. One
consumer requires independent certification; another allows deterministic
substitution. A provider returns a stale artifact after dependency drift,
shares across incompatible strategy or governance posture, drops the last
lease while work is active, leaks retained memory after cancellation, and
claims a cache hit as a new production occurrence. Independent execution,
dependency, occurrence, and resource oracles must expose each lie.

## Product Decision Lock

1. Reuse requires an installed equivalence contract naming identity basis,
   canonical dependency order, comparator, invalidation basis, provider
   compatibility, lifecycle, and consumer purpose.
2. Semantic artifact identity, production/acquisition occurrence, and
   independent-certification occurrence remain distinct.
3. Equal bytes or equal semantic content never manufacture a required new
   occurrence.
4. Exact, seeded, canonical-reduction, comparator/bound, distributional, and
   observational/non-replayable reproducibility are distinct contracts.
5. Query owns shared execution admission, owners, leases, invalidation,
   eviction, rebuild, disposal, and public reuse evidence.
6. Providers own physical caching and materialization behind the admitted
   lifecycle. A provider cache hit alone grants no substitution authority.
7. Reuse preserves the exact composite product branch and component bases,
   operation, strategy, capability, purpose, disclosure, verified footprint,
   attachment governance, and Milestone 9.18 correction meaning.
8. Stale, evicted, rebuilding, disposed, incompatible, or purpose-forbidden
   artifacts open no ordinary lane.
9. Cache reuse, certification replay, retry, redo, and ordinary continuation
   remain distinct.
10. Incremental maintenance and rebuild have declared profitable regimes and
    governed transitions.

## Phase Plan

### Phase 1: Equivalence And Purpose Authority

Install semantic equivalence, reproducibility, dependency, provider, and
consumer-purpose contracts. Deny substitution when any required basis is
missing or ambiguous.

### Phase 2: Shared Ownership And Leases

Create one framework-owned shared execution with move-only consumer leases,
exact fan-out accounting, cancellation posture, and terminal disposal.

### Phase 3: Invalidation And Lifecycle

Bind dependencies and governance to maintenance, stale transition, eviction,
rebuild, and disposal. No cached artifact survives a relevant semantic,
authority, provider, or policy change by convention.

### Phase 4: Research Adoption And Hostile Certification

Research workloads pressure equal-content distinct occurrences, independent
certification, comparator/distribution equivalence, and observational
non-replayability. Public facade, executable documentation, resource,
dependency, occurrence, stale-reuse, provider-parity, mutation, and residue
evidence close together.

## Performance Contract

- Reuse admission has a declared bound over canonical identity, dependency,
  provider, governance, and purpose facts.
- One eligible shared execution performs provider work once; fan-out adds only
  declared lease/publication cost.
- Retained bytes, lease count, maintenance work, eviction, rebuild, and
  disposal are separately counted.
- Background maintenance and reclamation are bounded and cannot launder
  ordinary latency or memory.
- The reuse path must demonstrate a profitable regime and must deny or rebuild
  when validation costs erase the benefit.

## Acceptance Evidence

Milestone 9.22 closes when equivalent eligible consumers share exactly once,
ineligible occurrence purposes never share, stale dependencies invalidate
before use, provider and governance incompatibility deny, leases dispose
exactly across cancellation and failure, retained resources remain bounded,
cache hits cannot manufacture production or certification occurrences, and
alternate providers converge on semantic and evidence meaning.

## Handoff

Milestone 13 consumes the provider-independent oracles exported by Milestones
9.19 through 9.22 and proves parity across the completed runtime-backed Query
framework. Later Store and cross-runtime milestones may supply durable
mechanisms, but they may not reinterpret the semantics accepted here.
